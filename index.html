Theory of Techne

The concept of techne, as borrowed from Ancient Greek philosophy, is the embodiment of knowledge and making: the "know how" and the "know that." It emphasizes the combination of theoretical understanding with practical application, especially in digital environments. While crafting my portfolio, I consciously aligned with the principles of techne, as redefined through the lens of digital writing and design. These principles include body/identity, distribution/circulation, access/accessibility, interaction, and economics. Each of these has been instrumental in shaping the structure, design, and intent of my portfolio. The body/identity is expressed through the way I represent myself in this virtual space. The headshot, which is very well placed, speaks volumes about professionalism and personality, bridging the gap between my online and real-world persona. The "About Me" section further reinforces this by detailing my academic background, technical skills, and multilingual abilities. By sharing aspects of my identity, I create a relatable, authentic digital presence that resonates with diverse audiences, including potential employers, collaborators, and peers. This deliberate representation ensures that my digital identity aligns with professional norms while maintaining a sense of individuality. Distribution and circulation play a significant role in how I structured the portfolio to facilitate seamless access to my work. Each artifact, be it the Pi-Hole network security system, the Enhanced PDF Chatbot, or my leadership experience in Tau Kappa Epsilon, is strategically connected and presented for maximum visibility. Using GitHub Pages as the hosting platform underlines an understanding of open-source ecosystems and their capacity for wide circulation. This ensures not only the accessibility of my projects but their integration into an ever-increasing network of knowledge sharing in new digital spaces. Accessibility has formed a key part of this portfolio's development, reflecting my commitment toward a more inclusive digital space. Indeed, I have striven for web accessibility by providing alt text on images, using responsive design on mobile devices, and utilizing semantic HTML for easier navigation. These choices ensure that my portfolio is accessible to people of different abilities without barriers. The contrast of colors, clear typography, and intuitive layout demonstrate an awareness of the diverse needs of users; thus, making my work more fair and effective. In prioritizing accessibility, I recognize the ethical dimensions of creating digital content. Interaction, as another vital aspect of techne, is reflected in the dynamic nature of my portfolio. It integrates smooth scrolling, modal pop-ups for detailed explanations of projects, and easy-to-use navigation menus that make for a great user experience. These features encourage active engagement, allowing visitors to explore my skills and projects with ease. This interactive design reflects real-world dynamics where technology has made communication and collaboration easier. By putting functionality on an equal pedestal as aesthetics, I ensure my digital presence invites exploration and connection. Economics, the last pillar, highlights the value exchanged by my portfolio. Presentation of my projects, leadership roles, and technical skills is a direct reflection of the return on investment I can offer as a professional. The deliberate organization of content, with high-quality visuals, speaks volumes about competency and capability. Using tools like Tailwind CSS, efficient and cost-effective, speaks volumes about resourcefulness and a results-oriented approach. In this sense, my portfolio shows not only my technical skills but also makes me an active contributor to the digital economy. The construction of this portfolio has developed my understanding of techne and its application to digital environments. The use of these principles in design choices has helped me create a professional, accessible, and impactful representation of my work. The process has taught me to bridge theory and practice by fostering inclusion and innovation through intentional design. This portfolio, at last, acts both as a testament to my skills and as a reflection of my commitment to ethical and meaningful digital engagement.

Accessibility Statement

This portfolio adheres to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 standards to ensure inclusivity for all users, including those with disabilities. Features include semantic HTML for assistive technologies, alt text for all images, proper use of heading tags, and keyboard navigation support.
