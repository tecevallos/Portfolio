Theory of Techne

The concept of techne, rooted in the Greek understanding of craft and knowledge, serves as a lens through which I crafted my portfolio. Techne embodies the blend of "know-how" and "know-that," merging practical skills with theoretical understanding. My portfolio demonstrates this through its design, content, and accessibility, while aligning with principles such as body/identity, distribution/circulation, access/accessibility, interaction, and economics. These principles are reflected in my key projects, including the AmogUS Origin project, the Human Rights Assignment , and the Vegans study, all of which illustrate how techne can create meaningful digital interactions. Body/identity plays a critical role in my portfolio, particularly in how I present myself and my work. The headshot, prominently featured in my portfolio, symbolizes professionalism and individuality, bridging the gap between my digital persona and real-world self. This concept is further illustrated in the Human Rights Assignment, where the representation of human rights violations across elections globally reflects the power of identity in a digital space. By connecting election integrity with digital platforms, the project underscores how technology can amplify the voices of vulnerable communities. Similarly, the Vegans study explores how individuals with disabilities use social media to challenge sociocultural norms, highlighting the interplay between digital representation and activism. These examples emphasize the critical role of identity in crafting meaningful narratives in digital environments. The principle of distribution and circulation is central to my portfolio's design, ensuring my projects reach diverse audiences effectively. GitHub Pages, the platform I use to host my portfolio, allows for seamless accessibility and visibility. The Human Rights Assignment exemplifies circulation by showcasing how internet platforms impact human rights awareness globally. The Election Vulnerability Index (EVI), featured in the project, reflects how data can circulate across platforms to foster understanding of election safety. Similarly, the AmogUS Origin project utilizes storytelling to share cultural insights and connect with audiences. These projects underscore my understanding of distribution as a tool to disseminate ideas effectively, fostering broader engagement. Accessibility is another cornerstone of my portfolio, ensuring inclusivity across devices and user needs. I have adhered to web accessibility standards, incorporating features like alt text for images, responsive design, and semantic HTML structure. The importance of accessibility is reflected in the Vegans study, which examines how marginalized communities use social media to challenge dominant narratives. The study highlights the need for accessible platforms that empower diverse voices, mirroring my commitment to creating an inclusive portfolio. By prioritizing accessibility, I demonstrate how techne can bridge gaps and foster equity in digital spaces. Interaction, a dynamic component of techne, is evident in my portfolio through its user-friendly navigation and interactive elements. The modal pop-ups and smooth scrolling features enhance the user experience, encouraging deeper engagement. This focus on interaction is mirrored in the AmogUS Origin project, which uses humor and storytelling to captivate audiences. The Vegans study further reinforces the importance of interaction by showcasing how digital platforms facilitate dialogue and community building. These examples highlight my ability to design interactive experiences that foster connection and understanding. Economics, the final pillar of techne, underscores the value exchange within my portfolio. The presentation of my projects, skills, and experiences communicates their significance to potential collaborators and employers. The Human Rights Assignment, for instance, demonstrates the economic value of digital tools in fostering transparency and accountability. Meanwhile, the Vegans study illustrates the economic implications of social media in amplifying marginalized voices. By leveraging cost-effective tools like Tailwind CSS, I showcase my resourcefulness in achieving high-quality outcomes. These examples reflect my ability to create value through thoughtful design and strategic communication. Through the integration of these principles, my portfolio serves as a testament to the power of techne in digital environments. The projects showcased within it illustrate my ability to blend theory and practice, creating meaningful and impactful digital experiences. By aligning with the principles of body/identity, distribution/circulation, access/accessibility, interaction, and economics, I have crafted a portfolio that not only reflects my technical abilities but also my commitment to fostering inclusivity, engagement, and innovation.

Accessibility Statement

This portfolio adheres to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 standards to ensure inclusivity for all users, including those with disabilities. Features include semantic HTML for assistive technologies, alt text for all images, proper use of heading tags, and keyboard navigation support.
